ThermoHeal-AI is a smart-foot-health and wellness-monitoring platform connected to a conceptual smart insole developed using banana pseudostem-derived cellulose/nanocellulose biomaterial, passive thermoregulation using bio-based phase-change materials (PCM), multimodal sensing, low-power wireless communication, and AI-assisted analytics.
