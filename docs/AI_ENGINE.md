> **LEGAL & REGULATORY NOTICE**:
> *Wellness insight only — not a medical diagnosis.*
> The algorithmic outputs, risk scores, and recommendations generated by ThermoHeal-AI are intended solely for general personal health wellness, pressure awareness, and daily trend tracking. They do not constitute, replace, or supersede medical diagnosis, wound classification, or physician care plans.

---

## 1. Architectural Philosophy

ThermoHeal-AI utilizes a two-tier AI architecture:
1. **Tier 1 (Current Production)**: Fully deterministic, rule-based clinical heuristic engine (`RuleBasedAIEngine.kt`) with strict versioning (`1.0.0`), explainability, and input validation.
2. **Tier 2 (Roadmap / Model-Ready)**: TensorFlow Lite (TFLite) edge inference pipeline for predictive thermal runaway and ulceration probability mapping, designed to slot into the exact same `AIEngine` contract without breaking downstream consumers.

## 2. Heuristic Scoring Functions

### 2.1 Peak Pressure Index (PPI)
Calculated from matrix sensors (Hallux, Metatarsals, Lateral, Heel):
- **Normal**: Peak pressure < 200 kPa
- **Elevated**: 200 kPa ≤ Peak pressure < 350 kPa
- **Critical Risk**: Peak pressure ≥ 350 kPa sustained over consecutive stance phases
- **Clinical Rationale**: Sustained plantar pressures exceeding 300–350 kPa are widely correlated in diabetic foot literature with microvascular occlusion, tissue hypoxia, and skin breakdown.

### 2.2 Thermal Hotspot Differential ($\Delta T$)
Calculated as:
$$\Delta T = |T_{\text{foot\_surface}} - T_{\text{contralateral\_foot}}| \quad \text{or} \quad |T_{\text{hotspot}} - T_{\text{baseline\_norm}}|$$
- **Normal**: $\Delta T < 1.1^\circ\text{C}$ (2.0°F)
- **Early Warning**: $1.1^\circ\text{C} \le \Delta T < 2.2^\circ\text{C}$ (2.0°F – 4.0°F)
- **Inflammatory Hotspot Alert**: $\Delta T \ge 2.2^\circ\text{C}$ (4.0°F) sustained across multiple measurement windows
- **Clinical Rationale**: Continuous thermometry studies demonstrate that localized hyperthermia of >2.2°C precedes neuropathic diabetic foot ulcer development by up to 7–14 days.

### 2.3 Microclimate / Moisture Risk
- **Dry/Optimal**: 30% – 60% Relative Humidity
- **Maceration Risk Zone**: > 75% Relative Humidity with high skin contact duration
- **Clinical Rationale**: Elevated stratum corneum hydration lowers tissue shear resistance by up to 50%, exacerbating blister and ulcer initiation under repetitive mechanical loading.

### 2.4 Gait Kinematic Asymmetry Ratio
Calculated as:
$$\text{Asymmetry} = \frac{|T_{\text{left\_stance}} - T_{\text{right\_stance}}|}{\max(T_{\text{left}}, T_{\text{right}})}$$
- **Symmetric**: Asymmetry < 8%
- **Mild Asymmetry**: 8% – 15%
- **Pathological Antalgic Gait**: > 15%
- **Clinical Rationale**: Patients unloading an injured or painful foot exhibit pronounced asymmetrical stance times, leading to compensatory contralateral hyper-loading.

### 2.5 Composite Wellness Index
The overall foot wellness score (0 – 100) is synthesized using weighted penalty aggregation:
$$\text{Score} = 100 - (W_p \cdot P_{\text{penalty}} + W_t \cdot T_{\text{penalty}} + W_m \cdot M_{\text{penalty}} + W_g \cdot G_{\text{penalty}})$$
- **Weights**: Pressure ($W_p = 0.35$), Thermal ($W_t = 0.30$), Moisture ($W_m = 0.20$), Gait ($W_g = 0.15$).

## 4. On-Device TFLite Evolution Path

1. **Feature Vector Formulation**:
   - 32-element sliding time window array (FFT spectral components of pressure + rolling thermal delta + moisture + cadence).
2. **Quantization**:
   - Post-training integer quantization (INT8) targeting Android NNAPI and GPU delegate.
3. **Safety Fallback**:
   - Dual-evaluation harness: TFLite model output is validated against `RuleBasedAIEngine` hard boundary clamps. If anomaly detected or confidence < 0.70, rule-based baseline takes priority.
